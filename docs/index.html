Open a Canary file

Drop files here, or use Open files…. Any mix works: a recording's parts (recording-…-part001.jsonl.gz, …part002…), a saved buffer (buffer-….jsonl.gz) or a report (canary-report-….zip). They're merged into one timeline.

They're in the server's UserData/Canary/recordings and UserData/Canary/reports folders.

Everything happens in your browser. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

What stands out

    Timeline

    Every chart shares one time axis. Drag across any chart to zoom them all; double-click or Show everything to zoom out. Hover to read values; the map follows your cursor. Everything else on the page covers the time range you're zoomed to.

    Update modules

    The game runs most of its per-frame work through shared update modules and times each one. These are milliseconds of main-thread time per second; a server running at 90 frames a second has about 1,000 ms a second to spend in total.

    Mods

    Files & time zones

    Canary stores every time as UTC. Each file's header says what the server's clock read and which zone it was in, which is what Server time uses. Your browser's zone is used for Your time.
